## Visual style ([brand] look)

**Aesthetic:** [brand aesthetic, one or two sentences].

- **Color palette:** [brand background color] backgrounds. [brand text color] primary text. [brand accent color] for highlights, headlines, key numbers. [brand emphasis color] for warnings and bear cases. [brand secondary color] for up-arrows and positive data.
- **Typography:** Slab-serif headlines (Roboto Slab, Recoleta, or similar woodcut feel). Clean sans-serif body. Numbers in tabular-figure monospace for ticker/data-readout vibe.
- **Slide treatment:** Parchment-paper texture overlays at low opacity on backgrounds. Subtle gold border/rule lines. Lower-third chyrons styled like financial news tickers but with the [brand] logo mark instead of a network bug.
- **Mood:** Slightly gritty. Mild film grain or noise texture. NOT clean, NOT corporate, NOT overly polished. Think "weathered ship's log meets terminal screen."

## Imagery & b-roll

- **Prefer real space footage:** NASA, SpaceX, ESA, Rocket Lab, JWST imagery — public-domain or fair-use. Launches, satellites, deep-field images, mission control rooms, hardware close-ups.
- **Charts and data viz:** stock tickers, earnings tables, candlestick charts, sector heatmaps. Style them in the brand palette (gold/teal up, red down). Animate sparingly — let numbers breathe.
- **Mascot inserts:** [character] can appear as a static logo bug in corner or as a transition stinger. Do NOT animate the mascot into the body of the video — NotebookLM can't keep a character consistent frame-to-frame.
- **Avoid:** generic stock-footage businesspeople, conference-room handshakes, fake hologram earth spins, AI-generated "futuristic city" imagery, blurry abstract gradients.
- **Apply a consistent color grade:** push blacks deeper, lift gold/amber tones, slight desaturation on greens (so the brand's gold + teal pop).

## Voice rules (narration)
- Cold open (15-30 sec, contrarian fact, drop in)
- Direct second-person address: "you," "watch this"
- [brand metaphor family], one per minute max.
- End: "[brand sign-off line]"
- No AI tells ("It's not X, it's Y" / "And that changes everything")
- No hype words (revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, leverage)
- No "So," or "Well," openers
- No generic-narrator filler: "In this video we'll explore," "let's dive in," "stay tuned"
- Vary cadence — short punchy lines next to longer ones. No AI-monotone evenness.

## Structure (6-10 min)

1. **Cold open** (15-30 sec) — contrarian fact + the question the video answers. Visual: bold gold headline number over deep-black background, fast cut to relevant b-roll.
2. **Why now** (30-45 sec) — why the audience should care today. Visual: timeline of recent events, dated chyrons.
3. **Core argument** (3-5 min, 3-5 beats) — narrator walks through it with occasional rhetorical pushback ("you might think X — here's why that's wrong"). Visual: one strong image per beat, supporting data slide, recurring brand-palette chart treatments.
4. **Strongest counter-argument** (45-60 sec) — narrator steelmans the skeptic, then responds. Visual: switch to [brand emphasis color] accent, "bear case" chyron, then back to gold.
5. **Practical takeaway** (45-60 sec) — what to do, what to watch. Visual: clean checklist slide, brand palette.
6. **Close** (15 sec) — tease next video + "[brand sign-off line]" Visual: [brand] logo + subscribe end card.
