## Side answer: multiple YouTube channels
One dedicated business Google account, each channel as a Brand Account (support.google.com/youtube/answer/4642409).
Separate emails give no protection: YouTube links "related channels" via AdSense/payment, identity and devices, and
making new accounts to evade a termination is itself terminable. Protection = no strikes. Add a second owner for
recovery; editors get Manager access, never the password. One AdSense per person [UNVERIFIED].
